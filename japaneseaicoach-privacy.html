Privacy Policy for Japanese AI Coach

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Japanese AI Coach is an offline-first JLPT N1/N2 practice app with bundled practice questions and listening audio. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information We Handle

The app does not require account registration and does not ask you to enter your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, camera data, microphone recordings, or health data.

The app may store learning history, answer records, bookmarks, review status, downloaded question data, downloaded audio data, and language preference on your device.

2. Local Storage

Learning data is stored locally in the app's private storage and device preferences. It is used to show progress, support review, resume practice, and remember your language setting. You can delete local learning and downloaded app data from within the app or by deleting the app.

3. Bundled and Downloaded Content

The app includes bundled practice questions and AI-generated listening audio for offline use. The app may contact Cloudflare Workers to check for or download additional questions and audio. Downloaded questions and audio are saved only in the app's private local storage.

4. AI-Generated Content

When server-side generation is enabled, the server may use OpenAI to create original practice questions and AI speech. The OpenAI API key is not stored in the app. The app does not send free-form user prompts or microphone recordings to OpenAI.

5. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob and related Google services may process advertising data, device identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, network request metadata, and related technical information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and legal compliance.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: advertising and ad measurement.
  • Cloudflare Workers: hosting, routing, security, and content download or generation endpoints.
  • OpenAI: server-side generation of original practice questions and AI speech when enabled.
  • Apple iOS services: App Store distribution, device privacy controls, system diagnostics, and platform functionality.

7. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information. Third-party services may process information only as needed to provide app functionality, download or generate content, display ads, maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for general Japanese language learning and is not directed specifically to children. The app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children without parental consent.

9. JLPT Notice

Japanese AI Coach is not an official JLPT application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the JLPT organizers. AI-generated questions and explanations may contain errors.

10. Your Choices

  • You can delete local learning and downloaded app data in the app.
  • You can delete the app to remove locally stored app data.
  • You can manage tracking and advertising-related settings in iOS privacy settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest updated date will be shown on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information shown on the app's App Store page.
